# Connecting Google Drive to Vero

You can bring files into your document vault straight from Google Drive, instead of downloading and uploading them. This page says exactly what we can see, what we keep, and how to stop.

## How it works

1. An owner or admin of your Vero organisation chooses **Connect Google Drive**.
2. Google opens its own sign-in page. You sign in there and allow Vero to use **only the specific Google Drive files you use with it**. That is Google's narrowest Drive permission. Google's wording also mentions editing such files: Vero only reads them. We never see your Google password.
3. In Vero, you open Google's own file picker and choose the files you want, and the company they belong to. Up to 10 at a time.
4. Only those files are brought in.

## What we can see

**Only the files you pick.** Google's permission for apps like ours (`drive.file`) only lets Vero open files you choose in the picker. Vero can't list, search or open anything else in your Drive, and doesn't try to. Picking a folder doesn't give us the files inside it.

Vero uses the permission only to read. It never changes, moves, shares or deletes anything in your Drive.

For each file you pick we read:

- **Its name, type, size and the date it was last changed**, so we know whether we can take it.
- **The file itself.** Google Docs, Sheets and Slides come in as PDFs that Google makes for us.

When you connect, we also read which Google account you signed in with, and keep only its domain (for example `kestrelpay.example` or `gmail.com`), never your email address.

## What we take

PDF, PNG, JPEG and plain text files, and Google Docs, Sheets and Slides as PDFs. Each file can be up to the same size as an upload (Google's own PDFs are limited to 10 MB). Word files and other types aren't taken from Google Drive: upload them from your computer instead. You can bring in up to 50 files a day.

## What we keep

- The files you picked, in your vault, marked as coming from Google Drive (which file, when it was last changed in Drive, the account's domain, and when we fetched it).
- The access Google gives us, locked with encryption, so you can pick more files later.

We never keep your email address or your password, and we keep nothing from files you didn't pick.

## What it means, and what it doesn't

Each file waits for you to confirm it, exactly like a file you upload. Bringing a file in from Google Drive is **not** a check that it is genuine, signed or legally sufficient, and it never earns a verified badge. A lawyer's review is still the way to know that.

If a file you pick is shared with other people, or with anyone who has the link, that stays your choice in Google Drive: Vero doesn't change it.

## If the connection stops working

If you remove Vero in your Google Account, change your password, or Google ends the access, Vero shows **"Google Drive access ended, connect again."** Connect again and carry on; nothing already in your vault is lost.

## How to disconnect

- **In Vero:** an owner or admin chooses **Disconnect Google Drive**. We ask Google to withdraw Vero's access and delete our copy of it.
- **In your Google Account:** you can also remove Vero at any time under **Security**, then **Third-party apps and services**.

Documents already in your vault stay there until you delete them. Nothing more is read.
