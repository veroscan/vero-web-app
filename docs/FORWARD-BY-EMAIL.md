# Forwarding documents to Vero by email

Your organisation can have its own private email address. Files you send or forward to it land in your document vault, waiting for you to confirm them.

## Getting your address

An owner or admin chooses **Create forwarding address** and picks the company the files are for. Vero shows the address **once**: copy it then, for example into your contacts. We keep only a scrambled copy that can't be turned back into the address, and the last four characters so you can tell which address is live.

If you lose it, or think someone else has it, create a new one. The old address stops working straight away. You can also turn forwarding off completely.

## What we take

- **Files attached to the email:** PDF, PNG, JPEG and plain text (`.txt`). Word files and anything else are not taken by email: upload those instead.
- Each file must be within the same size limit as an upload. Up to 10 files from one email, and up to 50 a day for your organisation.
- We check what each file really is from its content, not its name. Files with scripts or programs in them are refused.

## What we never keep

- The message itself, its subject, or anything written in it.
- The sender's full address. We keep only the part after the @ (for example `example.com`), as the email claimed it.

Nothing written in an email is treated as an instruction to Vero. And we never reply to senders, so a wrong or forged email gets no answer.

## Why every file waits for you

Anyone who learns the address can send to it, and email senders can be faked. So every file that arrives waits for a person in your organisation to confirm it, exactly like an upload, and nothing counts until then. A forwarded file is never a legal check and never earns a verified badge.

## Who can manage it

Only owners and admins can create, replace or turn off the address, and see when the last email arrived.
