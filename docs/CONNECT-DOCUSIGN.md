# Connecting DocuSign to Vero

You can bring signed agreements into your document vault straight from DocuSign, instead of downloading and uploading them. This page says exactly what we read, what we keep, and how to stop.

## How it works

1. An owner or admin of your Vero organisation chooses **Connect DocuSign**.
2. DocuSign opens its own sign-in page. You sign in there and allow Vero. We never see your DocuSign password.
3. Back in Vero, you see a list of your recently completed envelopes (the last 90 days, up to 50 at a time). **Nothing is brought in by showing the list.**
4. You pick the envelopes you want, and the company they belong to. Only those are brought in, up to 10 at a time.

## What we read

For each envelope you pick, and only those:

- **DocuSign's own status for it.** If DocuSign doesn't say it is completed, we bring nothing in.
- **The signed documents**, as one PDF.
- **The certificate of completion**, as a separate PDF, if DocuSign has one.
- **The date DocuSign says it was completed, and how many signers it lists.**

To show you the list to pick from, we also read each completed envelope's subject line and date. The subject is shown to you and never stored.

DocuSign's permission for apps like ours, called `signature`, can't be limited to reading only. Vero uses it only to read: it never sends, changes, voids or signs anything.

## What we keep

- The two PDFs, in your vault, marked as coming from DocuSign (which envelope, which DocuSign account, when it was completed and when we fetched it).
- Short notes about the envelope: that DocuSign says it was completed, the date, how many signers there were, and whether we got a certificate of completion.
- The access DocuSign gives us, locked with encryption, so we can bring in more envelopes when you ask.

We never keep the signers' names or email addresses, or the envelope's subject.

## What it means, and what it doesn't

Each file waits for you to confirm it, exactly like a file you upload. The notes say only: **"DocuSign's own record says this envelope was completed on (date)."**

That is not a legal check. It does not say a document is genuine, validly signed or legally enough for what you need, and it never earns a verified badge. A lawyer's review is still the way to know that.

## If the connection stops working

DocuSign's access lasts about 30 days unless it is renewed, and it ends at once if you revoke it on DocuSign or change your DocuSign password. Vero then shows **"DocuSign access ended, connect again."** Connect again and carry on; nothing already in your vault is lost.

## How to disconnect

- **In Vero:** an owner or admin chooses **Disconnect DocuSign**. We delete the access DocuSign gave us and our record of the connection.
- **On DocuSign:** DocuSign doesn't let us withdraw the access from our side, so to remove Vero from your DocuSign account as well, open your DocuSign profile, then **Connected Apps**, and revoke Vero.

After disconnecting, notes already made stay in your record, marked as from a disconnected DocuSign account, and documents already in your vault stay there until you delete them. Nothing more is read.
