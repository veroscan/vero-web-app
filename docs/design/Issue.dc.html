UrgentIntellectual property

Your lead developer hasn't signed the code over to Kestrel Pay

What's missing

You don't have a signed IP assignment from the developer who built your core product between January 2023 and August 2024.

Why it matters

Without it, the developer may still own the code. Your company may not legally own the technology it is selling.

What happens if you leave it

It will be flagged in your first investor due diligence, and it has to be fixed before any round can close.

What your contract says

Contractor agreement, clause 9

The Contractor retains all right, title and interest in the Deliverables and grants the Company a non-exclusive licence to use them.

What it should say

The Contractor assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in the Deliverables on creation, including all intellectual property rights.
